Habitile · Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026
Short version: Habitile keeps everything you log on your own phone. We don't run any server. We don't collect, see, or sell your data. The only optional cloud feature is a backup to your Google Drive, encrypted on your phone before it leaves — even Google can't read it.

What data Habitile stores on your device

Habitile keeps the following in a private SQLite database file on your phone, accessible only to the app:

  • Your username and a hashed copy of your memorable word.
  • Activity logs, expenses, monthly budgets, and personal targets you create.
  • The most recent quote of the day, cached so the app works offline.
  • Your preferences (theme, custom categories, etc.).

This data never leaves your phone unless you explicitly enable the optional Drive backup described below.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to enable backups in Settings → Backups, Habitile asks for permission to use a single private folder in your Google Drive (the App Data folder). Files in this folder are invisible in your normal Drive UI and unreadable by other apps.

Before any backup leaves your phone, Habitile encrypts it with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your memorable word. As a result:

  • Habitile (the developer) can never read your backups.
  • Google can never read your backups.
  • If you forget your memorable word, the backup cannot be restored.

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from the same Settings screen. Habitile only ever reads or writes its own App Data folder — never the rest of your Drive.

External services Habitile contacts

To keep features useful without any backend of our own, the app makes anonymous HTTPS requests directly from your phone to:

  • zenquotes.io and dummyjson.com — for the daily motivational quote.
  • open.er-api.com — for the currency converter's daily exchange rates.
  • drive.google.com — only if you've enabled Drive backup.

These requests do not include any personal information about you or what you've logged. They are identical to what any anonymous visitor to those services would send.

Permissions Habitile requests

  • Internet — to make the requests above.
  • Biometric / fingerprint — only if you enable biometric unlock. The biometric check is performed by Android itself; Habitile never sees your fingerprint.

Your control over your data

  • Delete everything at any time from Settings → Wipe all data. This erases the entire local database; no backup of it remains except (optionally) the encrypted file in your Drive App Data folder.
  • Disconnect Google Drive from Settings → Backups. You can also revoke Habitile's Drive access in your Google account permissions.
  • Uninstall the app — Android removes the entire on-device database. If you've never enabled Drive backup, no copy of your data remains anywhere.

Children

Habitile is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone — including children — because we do not collect data at all.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change what Habitile does with your data, we'll update this page and bump the "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes will be flagged in the app on first launch after the change.
